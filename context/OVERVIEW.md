# Context Overview

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

## What Context is

Context is developing a managed growth system built around educational webinars for B2B advisory and professional-services firms.

The current model goes beyond webinar production. It connects the full path from audience targeting and registration through attendance, live delivery, qualification, enrichment, follow-up, sales prioritization, and performance analysis.

The commercial goal is not simply registrations or leads. For advisory firms, the useful outcome is new conversations with qualified buyers.

## Current strategic thesis

Context's strongest thesis is that most firms do not have a webinar problem in isolation. They have a system problem.

A webinar can create trust and attention, but the program only becomes commercially useful when it:
1. reaches appropriate buyers,
2. earns meaningful time with the firm's expertise,
3. creates a clear next step,
4. captures useful buyer signals,
5. tells sales who is worth contacting and why,
6. routes everyone else into an appropriate nurture path,
7. learns from the resulting sales outcomes.

This makes the post-event qualification and sales-handoff layer especially important.

## The current webinar model

The methodology currently describes seven operational stages:

1. Onboarding and strategy
2. Creative and promotion
3. Media buy and optimization
4. Attendance and show-up
5. Webinar delivery
6. Post-webinar sales handoff
7. Reporting and go-forward review

The webinar presentation itself simplifies this into a buyer-facing progression around registration, attendance, event/content, qualification, follow-up, and analysis.

## Sales-handoff philosophy

Context's current back-end model enriches webinar attendees and evaluates them on **fit × intent**, rather than using a generic engagement score.

The working handoff model separates contacts into:
- already in pipeline,
- reach out now,
- nurture,
- research needed,
- disqualified.

Size alone should not elevate a prospect. A real buyer plus a reason to act now is more important than a large company with no signal.

The goal is to give the client's sales team a contextualized worklist rather than a raw attendee export.

## Educational-webinar philosophy

Current creative doctrine favors:
- clarity over hype,
- specific educational promises,
- genuine knowledge gaps,
- direct audience identification,
- practical takeaways,
- transparent selling,
- a deliberate transition from education into a defined next step.

Context does **not** treat “education-first” as “never sell.” The current webinar materials explicitly argue that the event should earn trust and set up a concrete next step.

## Important strategic tension

The current offer contains two ideas that should be kept distinct:

**A managed webinar service**
Context owns most or all of the operational webinar lifecycle.

**A broader acquisition / sales-activation system**
Context uses webinars as a mechanism for creating buyer signals, qualifying demand, and giving sales a prioritized next action.

The relationship between these two ideas is still being refined. Future strategy work should challenge whether webinar management itself is the enduring product or whether it is the first implementation of a broader signal-based acquisition system.
